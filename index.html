How we work

Nomitude designs and runs familiarization and press trips for tourism boards, airlines and DMCs. We handle the full chain: matching the right creators to the destination, from micro to macro, designing the itinerary, coordinating partners on the ground, and curating the content afterwards.

We bring together international creators and media in small, curated groups, so one trip reaches several audiences at once.

Partners receive full usage rights.
